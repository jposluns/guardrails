About and licence

About OPFiles

OPFiles is a neutral, self-contained operational-files standard. Its formal name is the AIQT Development Operational Standard; OPFiles is the public brand. It is owned by AIQT and published under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Ownership

AIQT owns the standard and is named in its title and brand. That is ownership, not a requirement dependency: nothing in the base-required vocabulary names AIQT or any other adopter, and a project conforms with the specification and its own checks. AIQT is also one profile, [profiles.aiqt], cited only as the reference enforcement suite and a consumer of the standard. See OPFiles and AIQT for that relationship.

The standard and the reference suite

The standard is the specification and quickstart. The reference enforcement suite is AIQT Guardrails, one consumer of the standard, whose source is on GitHub. A base adopter need not adopt the suite.
