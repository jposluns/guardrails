About and licence

About OPFiles

OPFiles is a neutral, self-contained operational-files standard. Its formal name is the AIQT Development Operational Standard; OPFiles is the public brand. It is authored and maintained by Jeff Posluns and is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2).

Authorship and trademarks

OPFiles is authored and maintained by Jeff Posluns. AIQT and AIQT Guardrails are trademarks of Jeff Posluns; AIQT is a brand, not a legal entity. The standard's formal name uses the AIQT trademark. AIQT Guardrails is one reference enforcement suite and one consumer of the standard, with [profiles.aiqt] as its optional reference profile. A base adopter need not adopt the suite.
