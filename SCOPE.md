# AIQT Guardrails: scope

This note records what the AIQT pack deliberately does not cover, so an omission is a recorded decision
rather than an oversight. Add your own rules for anything here that your context requires.

## Out of scope: fairness and non-discrimination

AIQT governs how an AI assistant does engineering work: accuracy, integrity, quality, trust, and
security. It deliberately does not encode fairness or non-discrimination rules for decisions about
people (for example hiring, lending, moderation, or access). Those obligations are domain- and
jurisdiction-specific and belong to the adopting organization. If your assistant writes or configures
logic that screens, ranks, scores, or decides about people, add your own fairness and non-discrimination
rules, owned by your HR, legal, or compliance function, alongside the AIQT pack.
