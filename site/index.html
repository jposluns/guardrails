Rules for your chat assistant, enforcement controls for your codebase.

Rules your AI follows. Controls that catch what slips.

An assistant sounds just as confident when it guesses as when it knows, and "done" often means the happy path ran once. AIQT holds it to 1 standard, Accuracy, Integrity, Quality, and Trust, above progress, speed, and cost: claims are checked or labelled, shortcuts are surfaced, and done means the stated bar was met. In Claude Code, an opt-in plugin of 30 hooks blocks or warns on the actions it is built to catch.(Accuracy = Integrity = Quality = Trust) > Progress > Speed > Cost

What it gives you

You cannot tell from the tone whether an assistant knows or is guessing. AIQT gives it 1 written standard: every claim is checked or marked as unchecked, shortcuts are reported, and done means the bar you set was met. You can then ask it to show its evidence and hold it to the answer. It installs in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and Copilot Studio, open source under the Apache License 2.0, except vendored third-party material, which remains under its own terms. Results depend on the assistant and the model you use. Its 30 Claude Code hooks ship as an opt-in plugin; a guided install and setup doctor arrive with 1.1.0.
