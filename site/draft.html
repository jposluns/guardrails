What changes

What changes when AIQT is on

An AI assistant can sound confident with nothing behind it, call unchecked work done, or change a detail between one reply and the next. AIQT holds your assistant to a standard on every one of them.

Your assistant shows its work

Every factual claim points back to a source, and when a guardrail catches something, your assistant tells you which guardrail and what it caught. You can see the reasoning, so you can check it.

It stops and asks when unsure

When your assistant does not know something, it says so plainly. When a decision needs your judgement, it pauses and asks. A plausible-sounding invention never fills the gap.

A "done" you can rely on

AIQT requires that "done" rests on a check that actually ran. Your assistant verifies before it declares, so every "done" comes with a check you can point to.

Nothing changes silently

No quietly weakened checks, no half-built results dressed up as finished, no invented names or citations. When something fails, you hear about it before anyone else does.

The standard

The standard, in plain terms

Four things you can expect from an assistant that follows AIQT. Together they are the top tier of the ordering, and they always come first.

Accuracy

What it says matches what is true. Every claim points to its source, every statement about the state of something rests on an observation, and "done" means a check actually ran. An unknown is stated as an unknown.

Integrity

The work is what it appears to be. Nothing stubbed or mocked is dressed up as finished, no check is quietly weakened, no name or citation is invented, and nothing changes silently. Failures are surfaced.

Quality

The work meets a standard of craft: correct against the requirements, consistent with the conventions, and complete across every surface a change touches.

Trust

Trust is warranted by the record and granted by you, one evidenced claim at a time. Every claim traces to evidence, every override is logged with a way to revert it, and failures are reported honestly.

Decided in advance. When correctness and craft pull against being fast or being cheap, correctness wins, every time. That call is made once, up front, so nobody has to re-argue it under a deadline. The four facets in the top tier are co-equal, with no ranking among them. Just below them sits Progress, decisive advancement: when the answer is derivable, decide and act. Progress never licenses less verification.
