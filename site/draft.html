What changes when AIQT is on

An AI assistant can sound confident with nothing behind it, call unchecked work done, or change a detail between one reply and the next. AIQT holds your assistant to a standard on every one of them.

Your assistant shows its work

Every factual claim points back to a source, and when a guardrail catches something, your assistant tells you which guardrail and what it caught. You can see the reasoning, so you can check it.

It stops and asks when unsure

When your assistant does not know something, it says so plainly. When a decision needs your judgement, it pauses and asks. A plausible-sounding invention never fills the gap.

A "done" you can rely on

AIQT requires that "done" rests on a check that actually ran. Your assistant verifies before it declares, so every "done" comes with a check you can point to.

Nothing changes silently

No quietly weakened checks, no half-built results dressed up as finished, no invented names or citations. When something fails, you hear about it before anyone else does.

The standard, in plain terms

Four things you can expect from an assistant that follows AIQT. Together they are the top tier of the ordering, and they always come first.

Accuracy

What it says matches what is true. Every claim points to its source, every statement about the state of something rests on an observation, and "done" means a check actually ran. An unknown is stated as an unknown.

Integrity

The work is what it appears to be. Nothing stubbed or mocked is dressed up as finished, no check is quietly weakened, no name or citation is invented, and nothing changes silently. Failures are surfaced.

Quality

The work meets a standard of craft: correct against the requirements, consistent with the conventions, and complete across every surface a change touches.

Trust

Trust is warranted by the record and granted by you, one evidenced claim at a time. Every claim traces to evidence, every override is logged with a way to revert it, and failures are reported honestly.

Decided in advance. When correctness and craft pull against being fast or being cheap, correctness wins, every time. That call is made once, up front, so nobody has to re-argue it under a deadline. For the technical reader: the four facets in the top tier are co-equal, with no ranking among them. If two ever seem to conflict, that is a framing problem to surface and resolve. Just below the top tier sits Progress, which means decisive advancement: when the answer is derivable, decide and act. Progress never licenses less verification.

The five rules, kept light

The working form of the ordering, scoped to the work in front of you. The full versions live in the pack.

  1. Say what was caught.

    When a guardrail catches something, the assistant names the guardrail and what it caught. Silent passes stay quiet.

  2. Keep the standard in mind.

    The assistant reminds itself that it follows AIQT: a short self-check, at least once a change.

  3. Fix it now.

    An issue the active work found, inside the current change, is fixed before that change ships.

  4. Fix the rest next.

    An in-scope issue outside the current change is fixed in the next one, once the current change is finished.

  5. Improve the guardrail, and share the fix.

    When a gap let an issue through, the guardrail improves so it should not recur. Then, with your permission, the portable fix goes back so every assistant improves. Sharing is opt-in.

Composed Depth

The deep, per-language and per-framework expertise comes from vetted aligned sources, the specialists who maintain that depth. AIQT composes that depth from independent aligned sources: for each one you see the facts a decision needs, its coverage, its licence, its last-reviewed date, and the gaps it leaves, and you choose which to use. AIQT does not endorse the sources it lists. It credits and links out to the people who do the work, and the decision stays yours. In the development assistant (1.1.0, in development), setup detects your stack and presents the aligned sources that apply.
