For anyone whose AI assistant has let them down

Your AI assistant bluffs.
AIQT holds it to a standard.

You have seen it: the confident answer that turns out to be wrong, the "done" that was never checked, the detail that quietly changes between one reply and the next. AIQT is one skill you add to your assistant, a file of instructions and reference material, that holds it to a standard on all three. With it on, your assistant checks its work before calling anything done, says plainly when it does not know, asks when a decision is yours, and never changes anything quietly. You get answers you can rely on, and a clear record of why.

AIQT is a set of written instructions your assistant follows, not an app and not a browser extension. You add it once, and it works in your everyday chats.

What changes

What changes when AIQT is on

Think of the last time your assistant let you down. It was almost certainly one of three failures: a confident claim with nothing behind it, unchecked work called done, or a detail that changed between one reply and the next. AIQT holds your assistant to a standard on all three.

Your assistant shows its work

Your assistant is required to back a factual claim with its source, or say when it cannot, and when a guardrail catches something, it tells you which guardrail and what it caught. You can see the evidence: the source behind a claim, the check behind a done, the named guardrail behind a catch.

It stops and asks when unsure

When your assistant does not know something, it says so plainly, rather than inferring an answer and presenting a guess as fact. When a decision needs your judgement, it pauses and asks, instead of filling the gap with a plausible-sounding invention.

A "done" you can rely on

AIQT requires that "done" rests on a check that actually ran. Your assistant verifies before it declares, so every "done" comes with a check you can point to.

Nothing changes silently

No quietly weakened checks, no half-built results dressed up as finished, no invented names or citations. When something fails, you hear about it before anyone else does.

The honest boundary

What AIQT can and cannot do

AIQT is a behavioural standard, delivered as instructions your assistant is required to follow. With it on, the assistant is required to check before claiming, cite before asserting, ask before deciding for you, and announce every change. Results still depend on the model, the platform, competing instructions, and the tools available in the conversation. AIQT does not make an AI infallible, does not technically prevent a model from erring, and does not replace your judgement, your organization's policy, or the law. What it changes is what you can demand and check: a claim with a source, a "done" with a check, a catch with a name.

Open source

Open, portable, and shared

AIQT is open source under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. The guardrails work across tools, and any improvement contributed back stays open under the same licence, so a fix one team shares can be used by every other team. Contribution is opt-in.
