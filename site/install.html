AIQT is a standard your assistant is required to follow, not a guarantee that the model is perfect: results still depend on the platform and model, and you stay in charge. What it changes is what you can demand and check. Both files it ships as are prose instructions and reference material only, with no executable code and no network calls.

What changes

What you can now demand

The irritations that brought you to this page are exactly what AIQT is built to curb.

No more confident bluffing

Your assistant is required to back a factual claim with its source, or say when it cannot, and when a guardrail catches something, it names the guardrail and what it caught.

No more guessing to please you

When your assistant does not know, it says so. When a decision is yours, it pauses and asks, instead of filling the gap with a plausible invention.

No more "done" that was not

A "done" rests on a check that actually ran, so a finished claim is meant to come with a check you can point to.

No more quiet changes

No weakened checks, no half-built work dressed up as finished, no invented names or citations. Failures are surfaced to you first.

AIQT keeps your assistant honest. It installs the same easy way, one skill you add to your assistant, and we recommend adding it alongside AIQT. Visit cleanlanguage.ai to install it.
