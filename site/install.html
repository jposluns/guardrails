You and your chat assistant

Add AIQT to your assistant. It is one copy and paste.

Your assistant sounds just as confident when it is guessing as when it knows. AIQT 1.0.0 fixes that, and it is available now. The whole thing is one skill: a single file of plain, readable text. If you can copy and paste, you can do this.

Here is what you are about to do

A few steps, none of them technical

You are going to get one file, copy it, and hand it to your assistant as its instructions. That is the entire install. There is nothing to download and run, nothing to configure, and no account to create.

  1. Get the skill. It lives in the guardrails repository as one file of plain text. Open it. You can read every word of what you are about to give your assistant, and we would encourage you to.
  2. Copy it. Select the whole file and copy it, exactly the way you copy anything else.
  3. Give it to your assistant. Paste it in as your assistant's instructions (some assistants call this a skill). Wherever your assistant lets you set its instructions, that is where this goes.
  4. That is it. Nothing else to set up. From then on, every conversation is held to the standard, and you can remove the skill at any time.

Worried about breaking something? You cannot. The skill is text, not software: it does not install anything, change anything, or run anything. It simply tells your assistant the standard it is now held to.

What you get

What changes once it is on

The irritations that brought you to this page are exactly what stops.

No more confident bluffing

Every factual claim points back to a source, and when a guardrail catches something, your assistant names the guardrail and what it caught.

No more guessing to please you

When your assistant does not know, it says so. When a decision is yours, it pauses and asks, instead of filling the gap with a plausible invention.

No more "done" that was not

A "done" rests on a check that actually ran, so every finished claim comes with a check you can point to.

No more quiet changes

No weakened checks, no half-built work dressed up as finished, no invented names or citations. Failures are surfaced to you first.

Beyond chat

Writing code?

The development assistant (1.1.0, in development) brings the same standard into your repository: it generates the file each coding agent reads and writes only inside its own directory. And a team can share one standard across every assistant it uses, on the Teams & Enterprise path.
