About

Where AIQT comes from.

AIQT began inside the GRC Library, a public documentation project whose corpus is written under an AI-managed workflow. The disciplines that kept that work honest proved useful well beyond one project, so they became a standard in their own right.

Origin

Proven in practice before it had a name

AIQT started life inside the Governance, Risk, and Compliance Documentation Library (the GRC Library), a public project homed at github.com/jposluns/grc_library. The library's corpus is authored under an AI-managed workflow, and that workflow needed rules to keep it honest.

Those rules are the disciplines you see across this site: every claim tied to its source, every check kept at full strength, nothing called done without a check that ran. They were distilled into a portable guardrails pack and applied first to the library's own work, in the same repository the pack protected.

Over time the pack grew into something other people could adopt independently of the corpus. That step is under way now: the guardrails are being separated into a standalone project, AIQT, and the GRC Library will then adopt it, replacing its own in-tree copies of the guardrails with the standalone pack and consolidating the remaining library-specific tooling into a corpus-management skill. This is a deliberate evolution, not a rebrand: the standard proved general, so it earned its own home.

Direction

How decisions are made

As the projects progress, decisions about AIQT's evolution are made by lead maintainer Jeff Posluns, and they orient toward one question: what will best help the world.

Open, portable, and shared is the working answer. AIQT is published under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0, contributions come back under the same licence, and the direction is chosen for public benefit rather than lock-in. A guardrail that travels between tools and teams helps more people than one that binds you to either.
