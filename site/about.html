About

Where AIQT comes from.

Origin

Proven in practice before it had a name

AIQT started inside the Governance, Risk, and Compliance Documentation Library (the GRC Library), a public project homed at github.com/jposluns/grc_library, whose document library is written under an AI-managed workflow. It arrived in four steps.

  1. Built where it was needed. The AI-managed workflow needed rules to keep it honest: every claim tied to its source, every check kept at full strength, nothing called done without a check that ran.
  2. Distilled into a pack. Those rules were distilled into a portable guardrails pack and applied first to the library's own work, in the same repository the pack protected.
  3. Proven adoptable. Over time the pack grew into something other people could adopt independently of the document library.
  4. Given its own home. The guardrails are now being separated into a standalone project, AIQT, which the GRC Library will then adopt, replacing its own built-in copies with the standalone pack. This is a deliberate evolution, not a rebrand: the standard proved general, so it earned its own home.

A sibling project

Clean Language

AIQT is not the first project to leave the GRC Library this way. Clean Language separated first: a free editorial standard that keeps AI writing precise, direct, and natural, spun out of the same library and now a project in its own right (its source is on GitHub). AIQT follows the same route, from a discipline proven inside the library to a standard anyone can use.

Direction

How decisions are made

As the projects progress, decisions about AIQT's evolution are made by lead maintainer Jeff Posluns, and they orient toward one question: what will best help the world.

Open, portable, and shared is the working answer. AIQT is published under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0, contributions come back under the same licence, and the direction is chosen for public benefit rather than lock-in. A guardrail that travels between tools and teams helps more people than one that binds you to either.
