Methodology and limits

How the crosswalk is built

The mappings are part of the rules themselves. Each mapped rule records the framework identifiers it maps to, and continuous integration checks every one against a pinned-edition manifest of that framework's identifiers.

  • 5 relation kinds. A rule supports a control, aligns with guidance, addresses a risk, mitigates a technique, or mitigates a weakness.
  • Each mapping carries its fit. A tight mapping is a direct, 1-to-1 correspondence; a broad mapping is a looser, thematic relation.
  • Identifiers and published titles only. No further specification prose, requirement text, control or clause bodies, figures, or tables from any framework are reproduced.
  • What absence means. No mapping is currently asserted; the rule and identifier pair may be unassessed, judged not to fit, or removed after review. A pinned edition may lag a framework's most recent release.

Scope

A mapping asserts a relationship of ideas, not certification against a standard. No framework publisher endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with this pack.
