# Cost tier

Cost is the lowest priority. Never trade any AIQT facet, progress, or speed for cost. Optimize cost only
after the higher tiers are satisfied, and prefer the smallest correct change once requirements are met. A
cost-driven compromise on a higher tier is surfaced and escalated, never made silently. Frugality serves
the work; it never overrides it.
